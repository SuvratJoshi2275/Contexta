# Dataset pipeline

COMI-LINGUA LID: `Sentences`, `Predicted tags`, and `Annotated by: Annotator 1/2/3` (token dictionaries with key/value/index). Training reads annotator 1, not unverified predictions. COMI-LINGUA MT: `Sentences`, predicted English/Romanized Hindi/Devanagari Hindi and three annotators' translations; NOT WSD labels. IIT Bombay/Samanantar: Parquet schema must be audited locally before pair extraction; supports translation dict `en`/`hi` or explicitly named English/Hindi columns. Unknown schema produces no pairs rather than invented columns. Preserve IIT official splits; Samanantar supplied shards are training-only. Candidate SHA-256 IDs deduplicate exact normalized English sentence+target combinations; additional cross-source leakage auditing is required before gold splitting.
